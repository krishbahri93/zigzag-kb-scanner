Glossary — how the KWM Auto Screener works

The life of a trade: Approaching → In Zone → Triggered → Active → exit at Target (trade complete) or Stop (setup re-arms and waits again). The Scanner tab shows the first three; trades entered on earlier days live in the Active tab.

The strategy — Nested Swings V2 (Daily · Long Only)

The setup
A sharp decline from a peak A down to a common low B that starts recovering. The decline's size sets exact Fibonacci price bands for entry, target and stop.
Nested trades: T1 · T2 · T3 · T4
One recovery can host up to four trades at once. T1 is measured to the peak nearest the low; T2, T3, T4 to progressively higher peaks — so higher T-numbers are deeper setups with bigger bands. Each trade lives its own independent life: T1 can be running while T2 is still waiting for entry.
Entry rule (what "Triggered" means)
A daily close above the entry band, with price above both EMA 9 and EMA 21, on volume at least 1.2× its 20-day average. All three must be true.
Exit rules
Target: price reaches the target band → trade complete (that T is done). Stop: a daily close below the stop → trade exits, and the setup re-arms to wait for a fresh entry.

Signals (per trade)

Approaching
Waiting trade with price within 1.5% below its entry band — an early heads-up to prepare. Hidden by default; tap the Approaching card to reveal.
In Zone
Price is inside the entry band right now. Watch for the closing trigger — this is the actionable moment.
Triggered
The entry fired on the latest daily bar — a brand-new system trade.
Active
Entry fired on an earlier trading day — the trade is running. Managed on the Active tab.
Fresh
This trade became actionable for the first time on the latest scan.

Scanner columns

CMP
Current market price (last close).
Day %
Today's move vs the previous close.
Entry / Target band, Stop
The exact price zones from the strategy's Fibonacci geometry.
Vol ×
Today's volume as a multiple of its 20-day average. ⚡ 2×+ is a volume spike — unusual participation.
R:R
Reward-to-risk from the worst entry: distance to target ÷ distance to stop. Higher is better; it's the default sort.
To entry %
How far price must climb to reach the entry band (negative = already at or above the band's low).
Confirmed
How long ago this trade first became actionable.

Active-tab columns

Entered / Bars held
The trading day the entry fired, and how many daily bars the trade has been open.
Since entry %
Plain price change from the trigger price to CMP — how much the trade is up (or down) since entry.
R
Profit so far in units of initial risk: (CMP − entry) ÷ (entry − stop). +1R means you've gained what you originally risked.
To target %
Remaining climb to the target band.
🎯 / 🛑 cards & labels
Trades within 3% of their target or stop get a label under the symbol and are counted in the two cards on top — tap a card to see only those.
⚠ amber row
Price within 3% of the stop — review these first.

Target Hits & Stop Hits tabs

Target Hits
Trades that reached their target band in the last 30 days — that trade level is complete. Gain % = trigger price → target band (the system's move).
Stop Hits
Trades that closed below their stop in the last 30 days. Per the strategy a stop-out re-arms the setup — it returns to waiting and can trigger again. The "Now" column shows what that trade is doing today (Waiting / In Zone / Re-entered / Target hit).
