Glossary — how to read this screen
The strategy (Nested Swings V2, long only)
A sharp decline (A→B) that starts recovering creates up to four nested buy setups (T1–T4). Entry is a Fibonacci band above the common low B; the target and stop are fixed bands from the same geometry. This page shows every stock currently at or near its entry band.
Signal: In Zone
Price is inside the entry band right now — the actionable moment.
Signal: Approaching
Price is within ~3% below the entry band, climbing toward it.
Signal: Triggered
The entry has fired per the system (close above the band) — the trade is considered live.
FRESH
This setup became actionable on the latest scan — it wasn't there before.
Entry band / Target band / Stop
Exact price zones from the strategy: where to enter, where profit is taken, and where the idea is invalidated.
Day %
Today's move vs the previous close.
Vol ×
Today's volume as a multiple of the 20-day average. ⚡ ≥ 2× marks a volume spike — unusual participation worth attention.
R:R
Reward-to-risk: distance to target vs distance to stop, from the worst entry. Higher is better; it's the default sort.
To entry %
How far price must climb to reach the entry band (negative = already at/above the band's low).
Confirmed
How long ago this setup first became actionable.
